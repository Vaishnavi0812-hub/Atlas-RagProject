# Q3 2026 Procurement Report

**Prepared by:** Northwind Procurement Office
**Reporting period:** 1 July 2026 – 30 September 2026
**Currency:** EUR

## Summary

Total direct-materials spend for the quarter was **EUR 6.8 million**, up 9% on Q2 2026. Supplier on-time delivery across all categories was 91%, below the 95% target.

| Supplier | Category | Q3 spend (EUR) | On-time delivery | Late deliveries (12 months) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vendor Alpha Components Ltd. | Hydraulic actuators | 1,120,000 | 67% | 2 |
| Helix Sensing GmbH | Sensors | 640,000 | 98% | 0 |
| Baltic Steelworks | Structural steel | 2,310,000 | 94% | 1 |

## Risks and issues

**Vendor Alpha.** Two of Vendor Alpha's last three HX-200 deliveries arrived late: the April shipment by 12 business days and the July shipment by 15 business days. Vendor Alpha attributed both delays to raw-material shortages at its Gdańsk foundry. One further late delivery within twelve months would constitute a material performance failure under agreement MSA-2024-017.

Because Vendor Alpha is the sole source for the HX-200, Procurement rates this a **high** supply risk. Engineering has been asked to estimate the cost of qualifying an alternative actuator supplier.

## Actions

1. Increase HX-200 safety stock from six to ten weeks (owner: Procurement, due 31 October 2026).
2. Begin a second-source qualification study (owner: Engineering, due Q1 2027).
3. Escalate Vendor Alpha's performance to the quarterly supplier review.
