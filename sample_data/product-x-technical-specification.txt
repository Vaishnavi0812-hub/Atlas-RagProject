PRODUCT X - AUTOMATED RAIL-SWITCHING UNIT
Technical Specification, Revision 4.2
Owner: Northwind Engineering, Rail Systems Division
Document status: Approved, 12 January 2026

1. OVERVIEW

Product X is an automated rail-switching unit that repositions track points on freight and light-rail networks. Each unit is controlled remotely from a signalling centre and reports its position and health every five seconds.

2. MAJOR COMPONENTS

2.1 Actuation. Product X uses the HX-200 hydraulic actuator to move the switch blades. The HX-200 is supplied exclusively by Vendor Alpha Components Ltd. No alternative actuator is currently certified for Product X; qualifying a second source is estimated to take nine to twelve months.

2.2 Control. The ARC-7 control board is manufactured in-house by Northwind Electronics.

2.3 Sensors. Blade-position sensors are sourced from Helix Sensing GmbH under a separate agreement.

3. PERFORMANCE

Switching time: under 4.5 seconds.
Operating temperature: -30 C to +55 C.
Mean time between failures: 42,000 operating hours.

4. DEPLOYMENTS

Product X is deployed in the following programmes:

- Project Delta: 240 units for the Contoso Rail freight corridor upgrade.
- Project Orion: 85 units for the Harbourline light-rail extension.

Any interruption in HX-200 supply directly halts Product X final assembly, because actuators are fitted before factory acceptance testing.

5. COMPLIANCE

Product X is certified to EN 50126 (railway RAMS) and manufactured under Northwind's ISO 9001:2015 quality management system.
