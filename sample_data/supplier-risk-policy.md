# Supplier Risk Policy

**Policy owner:** Chief Procurement Officer, Northwind Logistics Inc.
**Version:** 3.1, effective 1 January 2026
**Applies to:** All Northwind business units purchasing production materials

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Northwind identifies, assesses and mitigates risks arising from its supply base. It supports the company's ISO 9001:2015 quality management system and the EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive.

## 2. Single-source suppliers

A supplier is **single-source** when it is the only qualified provider of a component used in a Northwind product. For every single-source component:

- the owning business unit must hold at least eight weeks of safety stock;
- Engineering must maintain a documented plan to qualify an alternative supplier within twelve months;
- the supplier must be reviewed quarterly by the Supplier Risk Committee.

## 3. Risk ratings

Suppliers are rated **low**, **medium** or **high** risk based on delivery performance, financial health and substitutability. A single-source supplier with on-time delivery below 80% in any quarter is automatically rated high risk.

## 4. Contract linkage

Where a high-risk supplier provides components to products committed under customer contracts, the Contract Management Office must assess exposure to delivery penalties and contract renewal risk.

## 5. Responsibilities

- **Procurement:** supplier performance monitoring and safety-stock levels.
- **Engineering:** alternative-source qualification.
- **Contract Management Office:** customer-contract exposure assessment.
- **Compliance:** annual audit of this policy against ISO 9001:2015.
