# Master Supply Agreement: Vendor Alpha Components Ltd.

**Agreement reference:** MSA-2024-017
**Parties:** Northwind Logistics Inc. ("Buyer") and Vendor Alpha Components Ltd. ("Supplier")
**Effective date:** 1 March 2024
**Term:** Three years, ending 28 February 2027

## 1. Scope of supply

Vendor Alpha Components Ltd. shall manufacture and supply the **HX-200 hydraulic actuator**, a critical component of Northwind's **Product X** automated rail-switching unit. Vendor Alpha is currently the sole qualified supplier of the HX-200.

The Supplier shall also provide replacement seal kits (part SK-14) and firmware updates for the actuator control board.

## 2. Volumes and delivery

The Buyer forecasts a requirement of 1,800 HX-200 units per year, delivered quarterly to the Northwind assembly plant in Rotterdam, Netherlands. Deliveries are due no later than the 15th day of the first month of each quarter.

A delivery is considered late if it arrives more than ten (10) business days after the due date. Three late deliveries within any twelve-month period constitute a material performance failure.

## 3. Pricing

The unit price for the HX-200 is EUR 1,240, fixed for the first two contract years. From 1 March 2026 the price may be adjusted by no more than 4% with ninety days' written notice.

## 4. Business continuity

The Supplier shall maintain at least six weeks of finished HX-200 inventory and shall notify the Buyer within 48 hours of any event likely to disrupt supply, including raw-material shortages at its Gdańsk foundry.

## 5. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach on sixty days' written notice if the breach is not cured. The Buyer may terminate immediately if the Supplier becomes insolvent.

## 6. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Netherlands. Quality obligations are set out in Northwind's Supplier Risk Policy and the ISO 9001:2015 standard.
